Clover Lane is a friendly, independent guide for people who would rather shop small. We visit the shops, try on the clothes, and tell you what we really think.

Iowa City has a lively mix of boutiques, from tiny corner shops with a handful of racks to roomy stores with fitting rooms, music, and a very opinionated owner's dog. Whether you are looking for a statement jacket, a first interview outfit, or a gift for a friend, a good boutique can save you hours of scrolling and leave you with something nobody else owns.

We started Clover Lane in 2024 after one too many Saturdays spent walking into shops that did not fit our budget, our size, or our taste. Our editor, Maren Okafor, began keeping a notebook of every shop she tried. Today that notebook has grown into the three guides below, each one built around a question shoppers ask us all the time.

How We Review a Boutique

Every shop in our guides gets at least two visits, and we pay for our own purchases. We look at five things: the quality of the clothes and how they hold up after washing, how welcome the staff make you feel, how easy it is to find your size, whether the prices match what you get, and whether the shop has a point of view of its own. A boutique does not have to be fancy to score well. It has to be a place you are glad you walked into.

A Few Tips Before You Go

Visit on a weekday afternoon if you can, because small shops are quieter and owners have more time to help. Ask whether a boutique offers free alterations, since a small tailoring fix can turn a good piece into a perfect one. And always ask when new stock arrives; many Iowa City owners restock on a regular day each week, and the best items go quickly.
