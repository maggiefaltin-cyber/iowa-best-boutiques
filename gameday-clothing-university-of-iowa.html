Gameday Clothing for University of Iowa Fans

What to wear to a Hawkeye game, how to dress for an Iowa autumn, and which Iowa City boutiques can help you look sharp in black and gold.

Gameday in Iowa City has its own dress code, and nobody has to explain it to you. Black and gold show up everywhere, from the walk to Kinnick Stadium to the tailgates, the bars, and the sidewalks the night before. The tricky part is not the color. It is dressing for a long day outdoors in weather that can swing from warm sunshine at kickoff to a cold wind by the fourth quarter. Our style writer, Tessa Lindqvist, spent several Saturdays testing outfits from our favorite shops, and this page collects what worked.

The Gameday Basics

A good gameday outfit does three jobs at once. It shows your team spirit, it keeps you comfortable for four or more hours on your feet, and it still looks good in photos. Start with black, gold, or a mix of both, then add layers you can peel off or put back on as the day changes. Comfortable shoes matter more than anything else. You will walk farther than you expect, and stadium steps are unforgiving.

Quick rule from Tessa: wear one piece that is clearly black or gold, and let everything else be neutral. You will look put together instead of costumed, and every piece will work for other occasions too.

Four Outfit Formulas That Work

The Classic

Easy, comfortable, hard to get wrong

A gold sweatshirt, dark straight-leg jeans, white sneakers, and a black beanie. Add a black puffer vest when the temperature drops. This is the outfit most students reach for, and for good reason.

The Polished Tailgater

For the pregame crowd that cares about photos

A black knit top, camel or tan trousers, ankle boots, and gold statement earrings or a gold scarf. It reads as gameday without a single logo, and it transitions easily to dinner after the game.

The Vintage Fan

For anyone who wants to stand out

A thrifted black crewneck or varsity-style jacket, a gold tee underneath, and worn-in denim. Vintage shops are a goldmine for this look because older sweatshirts often have a softer feel and a better fit than new ones.

The Cold-Weather Commitment

For late-season games

Thermal base, a thick black wool sweater, a gold scarf, a warm coat, insulated boots, and wool socks. Hand warmers in your pockets are a small trick that makes a big difference by the second half.

You do not need to buy a whole new wardrobe for the season. A few well-chosen pieces from local shops can carry you through every home game. Here is where our team found the best gameday building blocks.

Before You Head to the Stadium

Stadiums usually have rules about what you can carry in, and those rules can change from season to season. Check the University of Iowa athletics website before each game for the current bag policy, and plan your outfit around pockets and a small clear bag if one is required. Our own checklist covers the rest.

  • Comfortable shoes. Choose broken-in sneakers or boots, not new shoes and not heels.
  • Layers you can carry. A jacket that stuffs into a small bag beats one you have to wear all day.
  • Sun and wind protection. Sunglasses early in the season, a hat or beanie later.
  • Pockets. Phone, keys, and a card are easier to manage when your clothes can hold them.
  • A rain plan. A packable poncho costs a few dollars and can save the whole afternoon.
  • Fabric that breathes. Cotton blends and wool handle a crowded stadium better than stiff, synthetic layers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to wear black and gold? No one will check, but it is the unofficial uniform. If you do not want to wear a logo, a plain black or gold piece is a classic way to join in.

What should I wear if I am visiting from out of town? Dress in layers, since the weather in Iowa can change quickly. A black or gold sweater and a warm coat will cover most situations, and a local boutique can help you pick up anything you forgot.

What is the best gameday outfit on a budget? Combine a basic tee or hoodie from Brightside Basics with jeans you already own, then add a thrifted jacket from Pennyroyal's bargain corner. See our affordable boutiques guide for more.

Where can I find a gameday outfit for a group? Marigold Thread and Foxglove Fitting Room both welcome small groups, and our best boutiques list explains what each shop does well.
