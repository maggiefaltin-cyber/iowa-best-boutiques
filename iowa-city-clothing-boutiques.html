Iowa City Clothing Boutiques

Shopping for something specific? Here are the clothing boutiques we trust, sorted by what you are hoping to find.

Clothing boutiques in Iowa City range from sleek showrooms to cozy shops that feel like a friend's closet. The trick is knowing which door to open for which need. A shop that is wonderful for graduation dresses may not be the place for a sturdy winter coat. Our style writer, Tessa Lindqvist, tried on pieces at every shop below and noted how they fit, feel, and wash, so you can head straight to the right rack.

Dresses and Occasion Wear

The Gilded Sparrow

Cocktail, formal, and wedding-guest dresses • $70 to $260

A small, edited collection with excellent fabrics and a same-week hemming service. Tessa's favorite find was a deep green wrap dress that fit a wide range of body types without needing a single alteration.

Marigold Thread

Printed sundresses and relaxed day dresses • $48 to $120

For brunches, campus events, and summer weddings with a casual dress code. The prints are bold and the cotton blends are comfortable enough to wear all day.

Denim and Everyday Basics

Saltbox Denim Co.

Jeans, chore coats, and sturdy tees • $55 to $165

Saltbox focuses almost entirely on denim and does it well. Staff measure your rise and inseam and recommend a cut instead of letting you guess. The straight-leg selection is the best in town, and the shop hems jeans while you browse.

Linden & Loom

Sweaters, linen shirts, and trousers • $45 to $180

The place to build a capsule wardrobe. Everything mixes easily, and the neutral palette means one trip can produce a dozen outfits.

Workwear and Interview Outfits

Foxglove Fitting Room

Blazers, blouses, and tailored pants • $60 to $210

Stylist Noor Haddad helps shoppers assemble complete outfits for interviews and new jobs. Booking an appointment is smart during career fair season in the fall and spring.

Vintage and Secondhand

Pennyroyal Vintage

Coats, jackets, belts, and denim • $25 to $150

Everything is cleaned, repaired, and labeled with its decade. If you want a wool coat that nobody else on campus will be wearing, this is your shop.

Menswear and Unisex Style

Tallgrass Threads

Button-downs, knitwear, outerwear • $40 to $195

Tallgrass is a calm, uncluttered shop with a loyal following among shoppers who prefer well-cut basics. Many pieces are unisex, and owner Eli Svoboda is happy to talk through fit and fabric. The flannel-lined jackets are a local favorite when the weather turns.

How to Get the Best Fit From a Boutique

Boutique sizing can differ from the numbers you know from big retailers, because many small makers cut their own patterns. Try on two sizes whenever you can, and sit down and move around in the fitting room. Ask whether the fabric shrinks, since linen and cotton blends often do. If a piece is nearly perfect, ask about alterations; a ten-minute hem or sleeve adjustment often makes a good garment feel custom.
